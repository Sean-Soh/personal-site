## About the game

Skull and Bones is an open-world naval action game by Ubisoft, led by Ubisoft Singapore and released in February 2024 for PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and PC. Players captain their own ship in the Indian Ocean during the golden age of piracy, building a fleet and an infamous name.
