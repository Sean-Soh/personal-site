## My role

I was a VFX / Technical Artist at DreamRocket from September 2015 to September 2016, and shipped Kutsuki Puzzle PIKO as the primary VFX artist.

## What I worked on

- **Game VFX.** Conceptualized, designed and produced the game's visual effects in Unity and C#, creating animated elements and integrating them with existing game data.
- **Technical art support.** Supported the engineering team by troubleshooting reported bugs and applying fixes.
- **Gameplay elements.** Maintained and optimized assigned gameplay elements in C#, and quality tested them.
- **Game design.** Took part in game design and game balancing.
- **Art assets.** Produced miscellaneous art for the game, such as characters and UI elements.
