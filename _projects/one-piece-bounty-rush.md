## My role

I worked on ONE PIECE Bounty Rush as a Technical Artist / Client Engineer at goGame, a subsidiary of SEGA, from February 2017 to May 2018.

## What I worked on

- **Technical direction** for rigging, 2D FX and sequences, and UI/UX, implementing the solutions in the game.
- **Screenplay dialog system.** Developed a full-feature screenplay dialog system workflow from scratch using VBA, XML and Squirrel for the in-house proprietary engine.
- **2D animation** assets for cutscenes and special effects, and improvements to their workflow.
- **Game flow logic** implemented in Squirrel and proprietary tools.
