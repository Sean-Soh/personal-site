## About the production

Talking Tom and Friends is an episodic animated series for Outfit7, with production work at Tiny Island Productions.

## My role

I was Producer at Tiny Island Productions from 2011 to 2015, alongside my roles as Technical Director and, from 2013, Lead Technical Officer. As Producer I led the team to deliver projects smoothly, managed budgets and resources across multiple productions, held quality to the agreed standards, and handled client-side negotiation.
