## About the production

At Southern Star Singapore I worked on Bottle Top Bill Season 1 and Raggs 3D animation, where my work spanned stop-motion material and its integration with 3D.

## My role

As a CG Artist I:

- Rotoscoped stop-motion material and cleaned plates.
- Developed tools on image processing software to make the team more efficient.
- Improved the stop-motion and 3D integration pipeline by finding bottlenecks and resolving them with new methods.
