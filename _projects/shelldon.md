## About the production

Shelldon is a CG animated series set in an underwater world, following Shelldon and his friends.

## My role

I was Technical Director on Shelldon. As Technical Director I was in charge of technical setup and troubleshooting, built a production pipeline that left more room for the creative process, established the in-house rigging system and coached the team on it, and led look and shading development.
